Contributions

Viscosity
Three-level resistance model (technical, cultural, semantic). A thin prompt in a high-viscosity medium loses. A thick prompt matches or exceeds resistance.
Reactive vs. Operative Ekphrasis
Hintze et al. (2025): 700 autonomous loops → 12 generic motifs. Remove the human, get convergence. Reinsert, get divergence. Falsifiable.
Descriptive Adequacy
A thick prompt is adequate when it pushes generation away from platform realism in ways answerable to intent. Without it, thick prompting is verbosity.
