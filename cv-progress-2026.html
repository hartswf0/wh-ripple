Dissertation

Operative Description in Generative Media

Claim: This dissertation argues that generative AI transforms language from a descriptive medium into an operative one by analyzing how text-to-world systems compile navigable environments from verbal instructions.

Method: Thick prompting — iterative, comparative prompting under controlled variation — as a disciplined interpretive method producing analyzable evidence about model tendencies and failure modes.

Contribution: A new media-theoretical category (worldtext), a repeatable evidence method (thick prompting), and a bounded corpus of documented prompt-sequence demonstrations.

Original Analytic Contributions

Viscosity: A Three-Level Resistance Model

Technical (temperature/CFG), Cultural (training distribution gravity toward stock aesthetics), Semantic (concept entanglement). A thin prompt is low-viscosity in a high-viscosity medium: the medium wins. A thick prompt matches or exceeds resistance.

Reactive vs. Operative Ekphrasis

Hintze et al. (2025): 700 autonomous prompt→image→description→image loops converge to 12 generic motifs — "visual elevator music." Reactive = autonomous, convergent, authorless. Operative = human-in-loop, divergent, governed. Falsifiable. Repeatable.

Descriptive Adequacy

What makes a thick prompt "thick" rather than verbose? Adequate when it pushes generation away from platform realism in ways answerable to intent, context, code, and consequence. Thick prompting without descriptive adequacy is prompt inflation. Descriptive adequacy without thick prompting is just philosophy.

Three-Regime Periodization & Substrate-Aware Aesthetics

Extending Bajohr's sequential/connectionist distinction. "The prompt box is not just a user interface. It is a miniature theory of media."
