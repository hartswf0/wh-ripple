Watson Hartsoe researches how language, media, and computation become worlds.

This is the public archive of a worldtext practice: a body of research, design, teaching, media experiments, interfaces, essays, simulations, and prototypes studying how descriptions become environments, how communities become world-builders, and how virtual, material, narrative, and computational systems shape what people can imagine together.
