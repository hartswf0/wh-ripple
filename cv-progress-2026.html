Dissertation

Operative Description in Generative Media
Generative AI transforms language from a descriptive medium into an operative one. When prompts compile navigable environments, the output is no longer imagetext — it is worldtext. The dissertation demonstrates this through thick prompting: iterative, comparative prompt sequences under controlled variation that produce analyzable evidence about model tendencies and failure modes.

Contributions

Viscosity Model
Three-level resistance: Technical (temperature/CFG), Cultural (training distribution pull), Semantic (concept entanglement). A thin prompt in a high-viscosity medium loses. A thick prompt matches or exceeds resistance.
Reactive vs. Operative Ekphrasis
Hintze et al. (2025): 700 autonomous loops converge to 12 generic motifs. Remove the human, get elevator music. Reinsert the human, get divergence. Falsifiable and repeatable.
Descriptive Adequacy
A thick prompt is adequate when it pushes generation away from platform realism in ways answerable to intent. Without it, thick prompting is just verbosity.
